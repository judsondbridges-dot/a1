Privacy Policy & Data Safeguards

Last updated and effective as of August 2026.

1. Overview and Scope of Policy

SupperPlateBay respects your privacy and is fully committed to protecting your personal data.
This policy explains how we collect, store, and process information when browsing our site.
We gather limited technical telemetry to evaluate fine dining readership metrics and performance.
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing brokers.
By accessing our digital publication, you consent to the data protocols detailed herein.
Our security frameworks strictly align with global digital privacy standards and regulations.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We collect personal data only when voluntarily submitted through our newsletter or contact form.
Submitted details such as your name and email address are used strictly to reply to inquiries.
Technical log records including your IP address and browser type are automatically cataloged.
This server information helps us optimize page speed, prevent fraud, and maintain uptime.
We store your information securely using encrypted database infrastructure in audited facilities.
All collected data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill operational services.

3. Analytics and Tracking Technologies

Our site utilizes Google Tag (gtag.js) with tracking property ID G-0LY0HY7L01 for analytics.
This tag gathers aggregated, non-identifying telemetry regarding visitor count and page views.
Analytics cookies allow us to determine which gastronomy guides and plating articles resonate.
You can disable cookie tracking at any time by configuring your web browser preferences.
Google processes this data in accordance with their established corporate privacy policies.
We regularly review analytics configurations to ensure no raw personal identifiers are logged.

4. Data Protection and Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your information.
All communication between your device and our web server is encrypted via SSL protocols.
Access to personal contact data is strictly restricted to authorized publishing staff members.
Despite our precautions, no electronic transmission over the internet is completely error-free.
We continuously update firewalls and server software to defend against unauthorized intrusion.
If a data security incident occurs, affected users will be notified promptly as per law.

5. Reader Rights and Contact Information

You possess the right to request access to any personal information we hold regarding you.
You may also request the correction, deletion, or restriction of your stored personal details.
To exercise your data privacy rights, please submit a written request to our editorial desk.
Official inquiries can be mailed to 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
You may also reach our data compliance department by telephone directly at +1-888-777-5845.
We aim to evaluate and respond to all verified privacy requests within thirty calendar days.
