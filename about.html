✦ ATELIER MISSION

The Heritage & Science of High Gastronomy

Stationed in Soho, Manhattan, SupperPlateBay was established to research Michelin-level plating geometry, artisanal stoneware clay glazes, dry-aging biochemistry, and sommelier vintage pairings.

✦ Gastronomy Mandate

Artisanal Thermal Stoneware Over Mass-Market Porcelain

In an era of rushed fast-casual dining, SupperPlateBay champions slow, elevated gastronomy: celebrating 1,280°C vitrified stoneware ceramics, house-fermented koji reductions, and seasonal farm-to-table tasting narratives.

Our team of Michelin-trained chefs, master ceramicists, and sommelier scholars evaluate dining experiences through empirical research: analyzing sauce emulsion stability, plate temperature retention, and palate harmony.

"Fine dining is an intimate sensory conversation—where the warmth of hand-thrown terracotta stoneware elevates every vibrant sauce reduction into a lasting memory."

✦ Culinary Standards

Our Four Gastronomy Standards

1. Vitrified Stoneware

1,280°C high-fire clay dinnerware retaining dish temperature for 20+ minutes.

2. Dry-Aged Koji

House-fermented koji inoculations imparting profound umami depth to dry-aged meats.

3. Sommelier Precision

Tannin and acidity balancing for flawless food and wine flavor harmony.

4. Zero-Waste Kitchen

100% ingredient utilization through artisanal broths, garums, and vegetable powders.
