Cookie Policy & Tracking Technologies

Effective as of August 2026.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or phone when visiting web pages.
They help web applications remember your preferences and optimize your browsing experience.
Cookies cannot harm your hardware or execute malicious code files on your computer.
We use cookies to maintain your layout settings and evaluate reader engagement metrics.
Understanding cookie functionality helps you manage digital privacy preferences easily.
You can inspect, clear, or block cookies at any time using your web browser tools.

2. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

We utilize essential session cookies required for core site navigation and form submission.
Performance cookies anonymously track how readers interact with our fine dining guides.
Functional cookies save user preferences such as active tab choices in interactive widgets.
Third-party analytics cookies from Google Tag G-0LY0HY7L01 help us improve site performance.
We do not deploy invasive advertising cookies or cross-site tracking profiles on our platform.
Every cookie deployed serves a specific functional or analytical operational purpose.

3. Google Analytics Integration

Our website implements Google Tag (gtag.js) analytics using container ID G-0LY0HY7L01.
This service uses first-party cookies to compile aggregate statistical site traffic reports.
Information collected includes page visits, time on site, screen sizes, and browser types.
All IP addresses sent to Google Analytics are anonymized prior to storage and analysis.
This telemetry allows us to refine article formatting and enhance mobile loading speeds.
You can opt out of Google Analytics processing by installing their official browser add-on.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to manage cookie permissions through their settings.
You can choose to block all cookies, accept third-party cookies, or clear browser cache.
Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain interactive site features.
Instructions for managing cookies differ depending on your specific web browser software.
Refer to your browser help documentation for detailed step-by-step configuration steps.
Continuing to browse our publication without changing settings implies cookie acceptance.
